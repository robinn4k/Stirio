sounding names, which scheming, intaginative penny-a-liners have given
them in order to make large volumes out of little material.

pilation of this hook, the hints, suggestions, recipes and commandments being

sweetening, a dash of orgeat may be added before shaking.

with cracked ice and water, and hold it high up over the glass containing the
absinthe, allowing it to drip until the glass is lull; then serve.

                                 OASKINESS.                                   318.
     Caskiness is caused by wine being put into a dirty cask or into one that
has been unused for a long time. It can be removed by vigorously agitating
the wine for some time with a little sweet or almond oil. The cause of the
bad taste is the presence of an essential oil, which the fixed oil combines with
and carries to the surface, from whence it can be skimmed off or the wine
be drawn off beneath it. A little coarsely powdered, freshly burnt charcoal,
or some slices of bread toasted black, or bruised mustard seed. sometimes effect
the removal of the objectionable taste.
