               How Wines get their Colour.


   Red Wine !s made from black grapes, and white wine from

the ski ns, consequently a curious fact is that while black

less famous golden beverage of Champagne. This result is
obtained by usin g special pressing tables, which separate the
juice from the skins of the grapes before fermentacion sets in ,
